GP summary

A one-page summary of your symptoms, ready to take into your appointment.

Walk in ready

GP appointments are short, and it's easy to forget half of what you meant to say. The GP summary tool will help you pull together:

  • What's happening: your symptoms in plain words
  • How long: when it started and how often it happens
  • How bad: pain, bleeding and mood on simple scales
  • The impact: days missed, plans cancelled, sleep lost
  • What you want: tests, a referral, or just answers

You'll be able to print it or save it as a PDF. It'll all happen in your browser, so we never see or store what you type.

Until then

  • Read how to prep for your GP appointment
  • Keep simple notes on your phone: date, symptom, how bad from 1 to 10
  • Bring someone with you if it helps. That's allowed.
