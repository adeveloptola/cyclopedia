The Cyclopedia manifesto

  1. Pain isn't a personality trait. If it stops your life, it's worth checking out.
  2. You're the expert on your body. Doctors bring the medical knowledge. You know what your body's been doing.
  3. No shame, no squeamishness. Blood, clots, discharge: we say the words.
  4. Periods aren't just for women. Everyone who has one belongs here.
  5. Asking twice is allowed. Second opinions, referrals, bringing a mate: all fair game.
