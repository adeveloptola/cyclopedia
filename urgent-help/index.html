Urgent help

If something feels really wrong, trust that feeling. Here's who to call.

Urgent help

  • 999 Emergency. If you or someone else is seriously ill, injured or in danger, call 999 or go to A&E.
  • 111 NHS 111. Need help fast but it's not an emergency? Call 111 or use 111.nhs.uk, 24/7.
  • 116 123 Samaritans. Struggling or having a hard time? Call free, day or night, or email jo@samaritans.org.

Call 999 or go to A&E if you have…

  • Sudden, severe pain in your tummy or pelvis, especially with fainting or feeling very unwell
  • Very heavy bleeding, like soaking a pad or tampon every hour for a couple of hours, plus feeling dizzy or faint
  • Signs of toxic shock syndrome, like a sudden high temperature, a rash and feeling very ill while using a tampon or cup
  • Thoughts of ending your life and you feel you might act on them

Other ways to get support

  • Shout: text SHOUT to 85258 for free, confidential support by text, 24/7.
  • Your GP: many surgeries offer same-day appointments if you call first thing.
  • A pharmacist: great for quick advice on pain relief and minor stuff, no appointment needed.
